## Regional read

The map is overwhelmingly a **southeast-suburban-collar** story, which
is exactly where our gettable/anchor Republicans lived:

- **Philadelphia collar did the heavy lifting.** Bucks alone produced
  three called flips (HD-18, HD-142, SD-6) plus a fourth pending
  (SD-16 reaches into Bucks). Add Delaware/Chester (HD-160),
  Montgomery/Berks (SD-24). The suburban R bench — our softest,
  most-gettable tier — did not get converted; it got replaced.
- **Lehigh Valley** is the second cluster: HD-137 (Emrick, an anchor)
  and SD-16 (Coleman, pending).
- **Outliers:** one western flip (Allegheny HD-44) and one Harrisburg-
  metro open seat (Cumberland HD-88, Delozier retirement).
- **The one D→R flip** is outside the collar entirely:
  **Lancaster SD-36**, where D freshman Malone (won a 2025 special)
  lost to R Tom Jones. But Jones is the former HD-98 rep and was a
  **reliable ADU (HB 2186) Yea** in the House — so on housing this may
  be a wash or even a **potential Senate champion** pickup, not a loss.
- **The deep-R-No rural regions didn't move** (North Central, Central,
  South Central, Southwest rural, Northwest). No opponents converted;
  the gains are entirely suburban.

**Coalition cost:** we lost **both** anchors that were on the ballot in
flip districts — **Hogan (HD-142)** and **Emrick (HD-137)**. Their
Democratic successors are unproven on these bills, so this is a loss of
two rock-solid R champions, not a swap for new Yeas — and Delozier (a
GG-Yea) retired. Bipartisan cover is thinner until the newcomers have a
record.

## Committee impact

Six committees touch housing/land-use and transportation. Below, who
leaves each **by losing** (defeat = "unelected"), vs. leaving by
retirement, vs. **pending** (uncalled, currently trailing).

### House Housing & Community Development
- **Lost (unelected):** **K.C. Tomlinson (HD-18, R)**.
- **Pending:** Milou Mackenzie (HD-131, R) — uncalled.
- Net: the committee's R side loses Tomlinson for certain; the incoming
  D majority will reconstitute it anyway (D-chaired, D-stacked), so the
  practical effect is a friendlier committee, minus one R housing voice.

### House Local Government (the MPC / zoning committee)
- **Lost (unelected):** none *called* yet.
- **Pending:** Brett Miller (HD-41, R) — uncalled, trailing.
- D members all appear safe (Freeman, Borowski, Donahue, Fleming,
  Haddock, Hanbidge, Kazeem, Madsen, Munroe — Munroe confirmed held —
  Probst, Prokopiak, Salisbury, Sappey, Smith-Wade-El).

### House Transportation
- **Lost (unelected):** **K.C. Tomlinson (HD-18, R)** (also sits here).
- **Pending:** Shelby Labs (HD-143, R) and Donna Scheuren (HD-147, R) —
  both uncalled.

### Senate Urban Affairs & Housing (the Senate zoning committee)
- **Lost (unelected):** **Frank Farry (SD-6, R)**.
- **Pending:** **Jarrett Coleman (SD-16, R)** — uncalled, trailing.
  Note Coleman had *cosponsored* a Senate housing bill (one of the few
  R committee members who did), so if he falls we lose an R cosponsor
  off the committee, not just a vote.
- Remaining R members not on the ballot / holding: Picozzi (SD-5),
  Keefer (SD-31), Argall (SD-29), Langerholc (SD-35).
- **This is the highest-leverage committee change for us.** It's the
  Senate gatekeeper that bottled up House-passed zoning bills, it now
  flips to D control, and the R who lost (Farry) — plus the R who may
  lose (Coleman) — are both off it.

### Senate Local Government
- **Lost (unelected):** none called.
- **Pending:** Rosemary Brown (SD-40, R) — uncalled, trailing.

### Senate Transportation
- **Lost (unelected):** none called.
- **Pending:** Rosemary Brown (SD-40, R) — uncalled (sits here too).

## Direct answer: who leaves these committees by being unelected

**Confirmed (lost their race):**
- **K.C. Tomlinson (HD-18)** — off House Housing & Community Development
  AND House Transportation.
- **Frank Farry (SD-6)** — off Senate Urban Affairs & Housing.

**Left but NOT unelected (retired — open seat):**
- **Sheryl Delozier (HD-88)** — did not seek re-election; her seat
  flipped D, but she chose to leave. Not on any of the six committees.

**Could join the "unelected" list if uncalled races hold (all trailing):**
- Jarrett Coleman (SD-16) — Senate Urban Affairs & Housing
- Rosemary Brown (SD-40) — Senate Local Gov + Senate Transportation
- Brett Miller (HD-41) — House Local Government
- Milou Mackenzie (HD-131) — House Housing & Community Development
- Shelby Labs (HD-143) — House Transportation
- Donna Scheuren (HD-147) — House Transportation

Note: committee *rosters* get rebuilt next session anyway, and with
Democrats now chairing both chambers' committees the makeup changes far
beyond these individual departures. The point of this list is which
sitting R committee members the voters removed — i.e., who we no longer
have to move, and which R relationships (Farry, Coleman) are gone.
