## Top line: the math improved, and the Senate is why

Our Yea bloc has always been the Democratic caucus (HB 2186: D 101–1;
the lone D Nay was Borowski, HD-168). The bills were never dying in
the House — HB 2186 passed 139–62, HB 2109 passed 123–78. They died
in a **Republican-controlled Senate (27–23)**.

Last night:
- **Democrats flipped the state Senate** for the first time in 30+
  years — net ~+3, to roughly **D 26–24** (2 of the claimed flips not
  yet AP-called).
- **House held and expanded** for Democrats — reported climbing from
  103–100 toward **~108 of 203** (9 uncalled).
- **Shapiro reelected** in a landslide.

Result: a **Democratic trifecta**. The strategic picture flips from
"passes the House, dies in the Senate" to "can move through both
chambers." SB 1281 (50–0) and SB 1279 (30–20) already showed the
Senate appetite was there once bills reached the floor; the chokepoint
was gatekeeping, and the gatekeeper changed.

## House: every coalition-relevant flip went R → D

Four Republicans in our crosstab universe lost to Democrats.

| HD | Lost incumbent | Crosstab bucket | Won by (D) | Vote-math effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 142 | **Joe Hogan** | **Rock-solid R anchor** (Yea on both) | Kristin Egan | Yea → Yea: tally unchanged, **lost an R champion** |
| 18 | KC Tomlinson | Conditional gettable (ADU-Nay → GG-Yea) | Bryan Allen | **Gain** — soft R → reliable D Yea |
| 44 | Valerie Gaydos | Slipped (Yea ADU / Nay occupancy) | Hadley Haas | **Gain** on occupancy |
| 160 | Craig Williams | Conditional gettable (ADU-Nay → GG-Yea) | Elizabeth Moro | **Gain** — soft R → reliable D Yea |

Three of the four are clean upgrades: soft or mixed Republicans
replaced by near-certain Yea Democrats.

**The one real cost — Hogan (HD-142).** He was one of only 14
rock-solid R anchors (Yea on *both* the ADU and occupancy final
passages). The raw count doesn't move — his D successor will also vote
Yea — but we lost a live Republican champion. That's a hit to the
bipartisan framing, not the whip count.

**Held R (no change):**
- Deep-R-No wall held — HD-39, 46, 58 all explicitly retained R. No
  gains from converting opponents; all our gains came from the softer
  gettable/slipped tier flipping blue.
- Anchor **Marla Brown (HD-9)** held — we kept that one.
- Also reported holding R: HD-8 (Bernstine), 14 (Kozak), 15 (Kail),
  28 (Shaffer), 57 (Nelson), 59 (Rossi), 60 (Major).

**No D Yea seats reported lost.** Borowski (HD-168, the lone D Nay)
not reported as losing — presumably held, no change to the one D No.

## Net read

Better, mostly because of the Senate.

- **Whip count:** House got modestly more Yea (three soft Rs upgraded
  to reliable Ds; Hogan a wash). Not a big raw-count move.
- **Durable change:** the trifecta. Bills can now actually move.
- **The asterisk:** our **bipartisan R bench thinned**. We lost Hogan
  and saw gettables *replaced* rather than *persuaded*. The next
  public memo's "this is bipartisan" case rests on a smaller set of
  surviving R allies (Brown HD-9 and the rest of the 14 anchors who
  held). Worth an explicit recount of the surviving anchor set.
