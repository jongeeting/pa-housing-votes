# HB 2186 — Accessory Dwelling Units: Voting Reference

**For:** PA Housing Choices Coalition member organizations + allied lobbyists  
**Subject:** House floor vote pattern on HB 2186 (Inglis), the ADU-by-right bill  
**Drafted:** June 2026, after the bill cleared the House on Final Passage  

---

## 1. Bill summary

**HB 2186** — Prime sponsor: **Rep. John Inglis (D, HD-38, Allegheny)**. 
Adds a new Chapter 63 to Title 53 of the PA Consolidated Statutes, making 
accessory dwelling units a permitted use by right in every PA municipality 
with zoning that allows residential buildings. Because it amends Title 53 
directly (not the Municipalities Planning Code), it applies to Philadelphia 
and Pittsburgh in addition to the rest of the Commonwealth.

### Floor vote history

| Stage | Date | PN | Tally | Roll call |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 2nd Consideration | May 6, 2026 | PN 3173 + A3220 | **134 – 67** | [palegis rc 1054](https://www.palegis.us/house/roll-calls/summary?sessYr=2025&sessInd=0&rcNum=1054) |
| 3rd Consideration / Final Passage | June 1, 2026 | PN 3373 | **139 – 62** | [palegis rc 1075](https://www.palegis.us/house/roll-calls/summary?sessYr=2025&sessInd=0&rcNum=1075) |

Two substantive amendments landed between the two votes and produced PN 3373:

- **§ 6302 per-lot cap.** Codifies that the "by right" allowance is for one ADU per lot or parcel.
- **§ 6305(14) short-term rental opt-out.** Allows a municipality to prohibit an ADU from being rented for periods of less than 30 consecutive days.

## 7. Muni-class composition of the Yea and Nay coalitions

PA classifies its 2,573 municipalities into legal classes under various state statutes. The classes correspond loosely to muni size and density tier:

| 2nd-class township | ~1,450 | rural / exurban townships, the vast majority of PA land area |

| D | Timothy Kearney | SD-26 |
| D | James Malone | SD-36 |

SB 1346 amends the **Municipalities Planning Code (MPC, Act 247 of 1968)**, which by its own terms covers 2nd-class A cities, 3rd-class cities, boroughs, towns, and townships — but **does not cover Philadelphia (1st-class city) or Pittsburgh (2nd-class city)**, both of which have their own zoning charters outside the MPC. HB 2186, by amending Title 53 directly, applies to all PA municipalities.

On the regulatory specifics SB 1346 is more permissive than HB 2186 for the munis it covers:

| Provision | HB 2186 PN 3373 | SB 1346 PN 1747 |
|---|---|---|
| Min floor-area floor | 200 sf | 150 sf |
| Max floor-area cap | 850 sf | 1,250 sf |
| Per-lot ADU limit | One ADU per lot | "At least one" — no max stated |
| 30-day short-term rental opt-out | Yes | Not in the bill |
| Ministerial 14-day approval (deemed-approved on inaction) | Not in the bill | Yes (§ 603.2(b)) |
| Written denial reasons + fix list | Not in the bill | Yes (§ 603.2(c)) |
| Utility connection-fee shield | Not in the bill | Yes (§ 603.2(k)) |
